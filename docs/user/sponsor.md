# Sponsors

Thank you for your interest in sponsoring. Start with the sponsorship
prospectus your event's organisers publish; the portal links it from the
landing page when it is set.

## How sponsorship is tracked

An organiser records each sponsor in the portal with its tier, its contact
and where it stands: committed, invoiced, paid. Sponsors do not need a
portal account for this; the organisers keep the record and send the
contract request from it.

## Donations

Where an event takes individual donations, they are counted toward the
donation goal on the dashboard and, when the organisers choose to publish
stats, on the public stats page.
