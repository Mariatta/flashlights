# Proposal questions

The proposal form always asks for the same core facts: the session's kind,
title, summary, outline, prerequisites, audience, level and language, and
the speaker's name, pronouns, bio, photo, location, affiliations and
handles. Anything else an edition wants to know is a **proposal
question**, added by an organizer on **Organize**, **Proposal questions**,
without a code change.

## Built-in fields

The top of **Proposal questions** lists what the form asks about every
session before the questions: kind, title, summary, outline, prerequisites,
audience, level and language, each with who sees the answer (everything but
outline, prerequisites and audience is public on the program, and those
three are too once the program is published). Two of them are the edition's
choice. Untick **Prerequisites** or **Language** and the field
leaves the proposal form, the organizer's session form, the speaker's own
session form, the session pages and the public program; a summit whose
talks are all in English has no use for a language field. Both are on by
default, and the choice travels with "start next year".

## What a question is about

A question is about **the session** or about **the speaker**.

- A session question is asked for every session proposed. The proposer can
  change the answer while the proposal waits for an answer, from **My
  proposals**, **Edit**.
- A speaker question is asked once per person for the edition, in the
  **About you** half of the proposal form, and stays editable on **My
  speaker profile**. Someone who proposes three sessions answers it once.

Questions are per edition. When the next edition is started with "copy the
speaker setup", the questions still asked are copied with the checklists and
rooms; the answers stay with their own edition.

## How a question is asked

| Asked as | What the person sees | What is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Short text | One line | Text |
| Long text | A paragraph box | Text |
| Yes or no | Two radio buttons | Yes or No |
| A box to tick | One checkbox; when required, it must be ticked (a code of conduct acknowledgement, for example) | Ticked or not |
| One choice from a list | Radio buttons, one per line of **Choices** | The choice |
| Several choices from a list | Checkboxes, one per line of **Choices** | The choices |
| A link | One web address | The address |
| Links, one per line | A box for several web addresses | The addresses |
| A number | A whole number | The number |

**Required** means the form cannot be sent without an answer. The **help
text** appears under the question.

Once anyone has answered a question, what it is about, how it is asked, its
choices and who sees the answer are fixed: the stored answers have that
shape, and they were given on the promise the form made. The wording, the
help text, the order and whether it is required stay editable. To ask the
same thing differently, switch the question off and add a new one.

## Who sees the answer

Every question says who reads the answer, and the form tells the person
answering the same thing in one line under the question.

| Who sees the answer | Who that is |
|---|---|
| Organizers only | People who run the program (the Organizers and Program teams, through "Manage the program"). Proposal reviewers, speaker liaisons and other program readers do not see it. |
| Organizers and proposal reviewers | The above, plus anyone who can approve and decline proposals. Someone who only reads the program (a Recordings member, a speaker liaison) does not see it. The default. |
| Everyone, on the public program | Published with the session, or on the speaker's public profile, once the program is public. Shown in the public API alongside the session or the presenter. |

The person who gave an answer always sees their own.

Answers appear under **Their answers** on the proposal queue, on the
session page and on the presenter page, each with a badge when it is public
or for organizers only.

## Switching off and deleting

A question nobody has answered can be deleted from the list. One with
answers is switched off instead (**Asked on the form** unticked): the form
stops asking it and the answers already given stay on the record.

## Copying from another edition

At the bottom of **Proposal questions**, pick an edition and choose **Copy
questions**. Every question that edition still asks is added here unless a
question with the same wording, about the same thing, already exists. The
answers are not copied.

## Affiliations

A person's affiliations (workplace, project, community, school) are kept
once on their account and shown with their volunteer profile and their
speaker profile. They are edited on **Update profile**, on **My speaker
profile**, and in the **About you** half of the proposal form, and they
appear on the proposal queue beside the proposer's name and on the
presenter page. They are not published: the public program and its API do
not carry them.
