# Terms of service

!!! note "This is a template"
    Flashlights ships no policy of its own: policies are the event's, and the
    instance links to them through `TERMS_URL`. Copy this page to your
    event's site, replace the bracketed placeholders and add whatever your
    organisation's own policies require.

Thank you for using [Portal].

[Portal] is run by [Organisation] for the people who make [Event] happen:
volunteers, speakers, sponsors and organisers. By creating an account you
agree to these terms, to the [acceptable use policy](acceptable_use_policy.md)
and to [Event]'s Code of Conduct.

## Your account

You are responsible for what is done with your account. Keep your sign-in
details to yourself; a one-time code emailed to you is yours alone. Tell us
at [contact address] if you believe your account has been used by someone
else.

## Your information

We keep the information you give us (your profile, your application, your
proposal, the files you upload) to run [Event], and we share it only with
the organisers who need it for that. Sponsors see your email address only
if you said they may. You can ask us at [contact address] to see or remove
what we hold about you.

[Portal] serves every page itself. It runs no analytics or advertising,
sets no third-party cookies, and loads no scripts, fonts or images from
other companies, so using it tells no one else what you do there. Your
information leaves [Portal] only through the services that run it:
[email provider] sends its emails and [storage provider] keeps the files
you upload. [If the instance reports errors to Sentry: errors are reported
to Sentry, without your details.] [If `SENTRY_SEND_DEFAULT_PII` is on:
errors are reported to Sentry with your account details and IP address.]

## Our side

[Organisation] may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms or
the acceptable use policy, and may change these terms; we announce changes
on [Portal] and by email to active accounts.

[Organisation]'s wider policies, where it has them, apply as well:
[link to policies].
